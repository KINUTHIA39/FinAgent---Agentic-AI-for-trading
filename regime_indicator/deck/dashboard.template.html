How to read this

  • Hamilton is jumpy and accurate — 181 episodes, median 5 days. It flips often and is right more often.
  • The agentic track is smooth and late — 95 episodes, median 21 days. It commits and holds.
  • Adding 89,380 market-wide news articles changed almost nothing. The panel's Brier moved 0.8337 → 0.8336 and it changed 2 labels instead of 4. The null result on sentiment is not an artefact of thin news data.
  • Look at 2008. The detector sat confidently in bear the whole way through, so nothing triggered the gate and the panel never convened across the biggest episode in the sample.
